Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Why should we choose VOLTX for our apartment?

We provide 100% free installation, high-quality ARAI-certified chargers, dedicated 24/7 customer support, and a completely hassle-free charging experience for both residents and the association.

Is the installation really free?

Yes! Eligible apartments and gated communities can get 100% free installation with zero upfront capital expenditure from the association.

Will the association have to manage the chargers?

No. VoltX handles installation, operations, smart metering, billing, monitoring, and regular maintenance so the association has zero operational overhead.

Can residents charge both 2 & 4 wheelers?

Yes, our multi-point charging hubs and dedicated chargers support both electric 2-wheelers (scooters/bikes) and 4-wheelers (cars/SUVs) seamlessly.

What about safety?

Safety is our highest priority. All equipment includes over-voltage protection, earth fault detection, IP65 weatherproof standards, and emergency shut-off controls.
